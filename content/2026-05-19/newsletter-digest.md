## Anton Leicht — Threading the Needle

### Cut Off 🆕 NEW
- **URL:** https://writing.antonleicht.me/p/cut-off
- **Published:** 2026-05-14
- **Stage:** analysis
- **Why it matters:** Leicht's first in-window post in five weeks lands precisely on the policy frame SaferAI has been building toward: that access to frontier AI is shifting from "abundant in the limit" to structurally scarce, and that three converging vectors — compute (marginal demand exceeds available chips), security (model theft, distillation, espionage — explicit DeepSeek reference), and US government posture (Mythos restricted to defenders before general release) — make this a durable equilibrium rather than a transient shortage. The piece reframes the AI-Diffusion-rule and BIS-controls debate as one not about *what gets exported* but *who gets access at all*, which has direct implications for AI Act systemic-risk methodology (a FLOP threshold matters less when the binding constraint on capability deployment is selective access) and for the IMCO/LIBE Anthropic hearing flagged in the last digest. Read in full before any forthcoming submission on access-conditionality or pre-deployment evaluation architecture.
