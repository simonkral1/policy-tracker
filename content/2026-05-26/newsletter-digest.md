## EU AI Act Newsletter

### #102: Pressure Builds over Anthropic's Mythos 🆕 NEW
- **URL:** https://artificialintelligenceact.substack.com/p/the-eu-ai-act-newsletter-102-pressure
- **Published:** 2026-05-20
- **Stage:** analysis
- **Why it matters:** Risto Uuk's backfill issue — the first since the 7 May Cypriot Presidency Omnibus provisional agreement — confirms three concurrent EU-side developments. (1) **30 MEPs from six political groups** have written to Commission EVP Henna Virkkunen warning that EU cybersecurity rules are ill-equipped for new AI-hacking capabilities like Mythos and that the bloc needs a "European mitigation plan"; IMCO has formally re-invited Anthropic to a public hearing, which the company declined at short notice. (2) The Commission's **draft guidelines for AI transparency obligations** under Article 50 (in force 2 August 2026) are out for consultation, and Uuk's editorial framing argues the binding fix is to require "a queryable record of what the agent did, with what authority, against which system" — an agentic-deployment transparency standard, not a chatbot-era label — which is directly relevant to any SaferAI submission on Article 50 implementation. (3) Commission spokesperson Thomas Regnier confirmed the Commission has held technical meetings with Anthropic since August 2025 and will "ensure to receive model access" once AI Office enforcement powers begin in August 2026, formalising the EU-side parallel to the now-collapsed US EO regime. The Parliament hearing-Anthropic-declined item is the single highest-priority near-term lever: SaferAI should consider whether to write to IMCO offering technical support for the hearing brief.
