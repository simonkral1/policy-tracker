## 📄 RESEARCH

- **When AI builds itself: progress toward recursive self-improvement** (Favaro, Clark — Anthropic Institute essay, 4 June) — the lab's first numerically grounded internal-deployment disclosure and a proposed coordination mechanism. Argues current AI-on-AI development is already producing the early dynamics of recursive self-improvement (RSI); recommends a coordinated pause framework with verifiability as the design parameter. **For SaferAI:** the most direct frontier-lab artefact yet that makes the AISI oversight-degradation taxonomy a near-term institutional problem. The verification framework Anthropic gestures at (compute-monitoring, training-run attestation) is precisely the kind of obligation Article 92 supervisory powers could carry, if the Scientific Panel chooses to treat RSI-precursors as systemic risk indicators. The political-economy gap — that an 8×-velocity lab is asking for the brake — is the principal opening for an EU/UK-led coordination push at G7 next week. [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/institute/recursive-self-improvement) · [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/06/05/anthropic-ai-pause-development-recursive-self-improvement/) · [SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/06/04/anthropic-calls-global-pause-ai-development-humans-lose-control/)
- **Automated Safety is Harder Than You Think** (arXiv 2605.06390, May 2026 preprint) — argues scalable-oversight techniques are still bound by human judgment calls on fuzzy criteria; downstream-deployer self-attestation cannot substitute. Direct input to the GPAI CoP scalable-oversight clauses. [arxiv](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.06390v1)
- **ForesightSafety Bench: A Frontier Risk Evaluation and Governance Framework** (Tong et al., arXiv 2602.14135) — 7-pillar systemic-risk benchmark covering agentic autonomy, AI4Science, embodied, social and catastrophic / existential risks; safety-capability evaluation finds "widespread safety vulnerabilities of frontier AI" across pillars. Useful taxonomy for any Scientific Panel methodology input. [arxiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.14135)
- **Dark Speculation: Combining Qualitative and Quantitative Understanding in Frontier AI Risk Analysis** (arXiv 2511.21838) — methodological piece on combining qualitative scenario work with quantitative risk decomposition; relevant for the Scientific Panel and AISI Trends Report next-cycle methodology. [arxiv](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2511.21838)

## 💡 EDITORIAL

The week's pattern is institutional scaffolding catching up to the lab disclosures. The EU has now both an Omnibus path and a Scientific Panel — the legal authority and the technical body that will use it — in the same fortnight a frontier lab publicly conceded that 80%+ of its production code is model-generated and asked for an internationally verifiable brake. The transatlantic question stops being whether supervisory architecture exists and starts being whether the EU's statutory channel, the US's voluntary-plus-vertical channel (the 2 June EO plus the 5 June biosecurity ask), and the UK's sandbox-by-statute channel produce mutually legible model thresholds before August. G7 Évian and Strasbourg landing in the same week make this the highest-bandwidth political moment of the cycle so far — and the first cycle where the most consequential safety artefact came from a frontier lab rather than from a regulator.
