## 🔴 ACT NOW

### AI Office external-evaluator workshop — participant invitations sent 7 July; workshop 15 July (follow-up)
- **Organiser / Jurisdiction:** European AI Office — EU
- **Stage:** draft (operational implementation of GPAI Code of Practice)
- **Deadline:** 2026-07-07 (invitations) · 2026-07-15 (workshop, online)
- **Sources:** [AI Office call](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/events/call-participants-workshop-qualification-requirements-external-evaluators-gpai-models-systemic-risk) · [AI Office page](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/ai-office)
- **Why it matters:** Independence and qualification standards for organisations external to GPAI providers that conduct model evaluations and assess systemic risk under the Code of Practice. This is now the single most operationally-live EU lever against selective-access regimes on the US side — if the workshop produces criteria that specify access depth (raw chain-of-thought, rail-free variants, internal incident logs) alongside institutional independence, the Article 92 supervisory architecture has a counterweight to executive-picked evaluators; if it produces institutional criteria only, the EU imports the US pattern by default.

### Digital Omnibus on AI — signing 8 July, OJ publication 18–25 July, entry-into-force day 3 after publication (follow-up)
- **Organiser / Jurisdiction:** European Parliament + Council + Commission — EU
- **Stage:** adopted (29 June); publication window open
- **Deadline:** 2026-07-08 (signing) · 2026-07-18 to 2026-07-25 (OJ)
- **Sources:** [Council 29 June](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2026/05/07/artificial-intelligence-council-and-parliament-agree-to-simplify-and-streamline-rules/) · [noze publication window analysis](https://www.noze.it/en/insights/ai-act-omnibus-adozione/) · [techjacksolutions on Council adoption](https://techjacksolutions.com/ai-brief/eu-ai-act-omnibus-vii-council-adoption-deadlines/)
- **Why it matters:** If OJ publication lands after 2 August, the pre-Omnibus Article 6 / Annex III text applies from that date until the third day after publication — a two-to-four-week supervisory-authority gap in which the AI Office would be nominally enforcing rules the Council has already amended. Publication before 24 July avoids the gap entirely. This is now the single dependency between the amended text and clean enforcement onset.

## 💡 EDITORIAL

The EU legal architecture consolidated when the Council formally adopted the Omnibus on 29 June, sending the amended text into publication and locking the Article 92 / 93 / 101 GPAI supervisory machinery in ahead of 2 August enforcement. The US selective-release architecture consolidated when Commerce lifted the Mythos and Fable 5 export controls under a "significant progress" letter that both closes the first executive-shutdown cycle and preserves the executive authority behind it — with the OpenAI 5 % federal-stake talks arriving three days later as the ownership-side extension of the same pattern. And the frontier-lab market structure consolidated when Anthropic released Sonnet 5 as a near-Opus agentic default at Sonnet pricing, three days after Glasswing widened to 150 organisations across 15 countries — a lab pricing Opus-adjacent capability into the enterprise agent stack while its most capable coding model runs under a partner-access regime the EU cannot yet see into. The political question for SaferAI through July is compact: whether the AI Office's 15 July external-evaluator workshop produces qualification criteria — access depth, raw chain-of-thought authority, reproducibility artefacts — sharp enough to matter *before* the White House voluntary frontier-release framework announcement expected next week names the US procedural counterpart. The 40-author cross-lab CoT monitorability paper and the Anthropic Sonnet 5 system card are the two artefacts sitting on the same table. First UN Global Dialogue on AI Governance opens today in Geneva; the three consolidations arrive at that room already complete.
