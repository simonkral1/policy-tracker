## 🔑 NEED TO KNOW

- **EU AI Office sends first Article 91 RFIs to more than thirty GPAI providers (1 September); OpenAI, Anthropic and Google among safety-and-security-track recipients; up to €15M / 3% of turnover for misleading replies 🆕** — Four weeks and one day after GPAI enforcement powers activated, the AI Office issued its first formal Requests for Information under Article 91. Two distinct tracks: the first concerns safety and security of the most advanced models — recipients include OpenAI, Anthropic and Google — and asks providers to show how they defend models against attack, whether independent experts have evaluated them, how they monitor systems post-deployment, and what their training data contains; the second concerns copyright and transparency and targets companies that have not taken part in informal compliance dialogues with the Office. Article 101 authorises Commission fines of up to €15M or 3% of worldwide annual turnover, whichever is higher, for incomplete, incorrect or misleading replies. Lands the same day the CoE Framework Convention enters into force for the Union, and the same week the G20 endorses a light-touch counter-framework. ## 💡 EDITORIAL

Week five was the week the enforcement era became crowded rather than empty. In a single 96 hours Brussels used the instrument, the G20 endorsed a framework designed to prevent instruments like it, US federal legislators proposed to shut development off entirely, and covered labs published incident records they had every incentive to keep. The transatlantic-supervisory-divergence framing of weeks one through four now has four axes rather than two: the AI Office is acting; Washington's executive is asking other governments not to; Washington's legislature contains a moratorium bill; and the covered labs themselves are shipping capability upgrades with named alignment failures attached. No prior week has stacked this many contradictory answers to "who has evaluator authority" in this compact a window. The reading for SaferAI is that the supervisory question is no longer whether authority will crystallise but along which axis it will — and the answer to that is now being written into Article 91 reply files, system cards, and joint-signed independent investigations rather than framework documents.
