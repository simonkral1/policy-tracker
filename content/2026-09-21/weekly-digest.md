On 18 September Anthropic named Accenture as its first embedded evaluator under Amodei's 12 September commitment; each firm pledged at least $1B over five years, with Accenture's Faculty division embedding at Anthropic with 'access comparable to an employee's' to evaluate, red-team and assess alignment.
