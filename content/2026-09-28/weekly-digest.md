Both cards frame the release as an incremental step against Fable 5.1 / Mythos 5.1 (Anthropic) and the GPT-6 Astra baseline (OpenAI) — the first covered-lab releases since Amodei's 12 September 'Pace the Frontier' essay, and the first releases inside the Article 91 first-round reply window. The AI Office's Article 91 first-round reply windows began closing across the week, and NIST SP 800-239 public comment closed 25 September.
